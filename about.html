About Hotel Medallion

A residence, not a room.

Hotel Medallion is a modern boutique hotel located in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos. It is carefully crafted and tastefully furnished to provide guests with a clean, tranquil, home-like environment rather than a temporary space. Medallion caters to travellers seeking the comfort and familiarity of home combined with the service and standards of a top-tier hotel — including returning Nigerians abroad, business visitors, and guests who prefer to feel cared for rather than just checked in.

In addition to accommodation, Medallion operates a comprehensive residence offering amenities such as the Medallion Bar, a 24-hour restaurant with a contemporary European-inspired menu, round-the-clock laundry services, and on-demand car hire — ensuring guests rarely need to leave the property to receive excellent service.

Our Story

Lagos has hotels. Not enough residences.

Hotel Medallion was built on a simple observation: Lagos has hotels, but too few residences — places where a guest, especially one coming home from abroad or in the city for serious business, can settle rather than merely stay.

Hotel Medallion was established in Lekki Phase 1 to close that gap: a boutique property with the intimacy and attentiveness of a real home, and the polish and consistency guests expect from an international-standard hotel.
