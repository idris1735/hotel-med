About Hotel Medallion

For business. For leisure. For Lagos.

Hotel Medallion is a boutique hotel just off Admiralty Way in Lekki, Lagos, designed for guests who value comfort, convenience, and true hospitality. Whether you are in Lagos for business, a weekend getaway, an event, or a well-deserved break, we want every guest to feel at home from the moment they arrive.

Rooms, a fully fitted gym, a swimming pool, spaces for events and meetings, the Medallion Bar, and an ultra-modern restaurant. 24-hour laundry and on-demand car hire, so guests rarely need to leave the property. Welcome to Hotel Medallion. Welcome home.

Our Story

Many hotels. Not enough houses.

Hotel Medallion was established on the premise that although there are many hotels in Lagos, there are not enough houses where guests — particularly those who have come from abroad, or to the city for important business — can genuinely settle rather than merely stay.

Medallion Hospitality Services was founded in Lekki Phase 1 to meet this demand: a boutique hotel with the comfort and personal attention of a home, and the professionalism and reliability of an international-standard hotel.
