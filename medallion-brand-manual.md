# HOTEL MEDALLION — BRAND MANUAL

*"A Quiet Kind of Luxury."*

> A brand guide sets out the rules and principles a brand uses to present itself, covering areas such as its vision, mission, promise, personality, tone of voice, messaging, and visual identity. It serves as a reference for Hotel Medallion's leadership, staff, and any partners or vendors who represent the brand.

## About Us

Hotel Medallion is a boutique hotel just off Admiralty Way in Lekki, Lagos, designed for customers who value comfort, convenience, and true hospitality. Whether you are in Lagos for business, a weekend getaway, an event, or a well-deserved break, we want every guest to feel at home from the moment they arrive.

The hotel is located in one of Lagos's most vibrant, easily accessible areas and offers rooms, a range of facilities, friendly service, and modern amenities. Guests have access to a fully fitted gym, a swimming pool, separate areas for events and meetings, and dining options such as the Medallion Bar and an ultra-modern restaurant. Other services, including 24-hour laundry and on-demand car hire, are available, so guests usually don't have to leave the property to meet their needs.

What really makes the Hotel Medallion stand out is its staff, because excellent hospitality is personal; the staff welcome guests with warm greetings and carefully prepare each room, ensuring every stay is comfortable, memorable, and uniquely Medallion.

**For business. For leisure. For Lagos. Welcome to Hotel Medallion. Welcome home.**

## Our Story

Hotel Medallion was established on the premise that although there are many hotels in Lagos, there are not enough houses where guests, particularly those who have come from abroad or to the city for important business, can genuinely settle rather than merely stay temporarily.

Medallion Hospitality Services was established in Lekki Phase 1 to meet this demand; it is a boutique hotel that provides the comfort and personal attention of a home, along with the professionalism and reliability of an international-standard hotel.

## Brand Personality

Medallion is the composed and attentive host, a friend who always has a guest room ready. Medallion is:

- **Gracious** — warm without being intrusive
- **Meticulous** — nothing left unfinished or unclean
- **Composed** — calm, unhurried, dependable
- **Discreet** — professional, private, trustworthy with a guest's time and space

## Brand Archetype

**Medallion is primarily a CAREGIVER brand, with RULER as a secondary influence.**

Caregiver brands focus on looking after people and are known for their kindness, generosity, and protective nature; their aim is to make individuals feel safe and cared for, embodying Medallion's spirit by going beyond simple accommodation to offer a genuine home.

The impact of The Ruler is evident in Medallion's elegance and the high standards it maintains, allowing it to be regarded as a boutique hotel setting benchmarks for quality and order in its class, just as a well-kept house reflects the standards of its owner.

## Core Values: G.O.L.D.

Medallion's values are based on the concept implied by the name: since a medallion signals that a standard has been met, GOLD serves as both the acronym and that standard.

- **G: Graciousness** — each guest is regarded as a welcome visitor rather than as a booking reference.
- **O: Ownership** — each staff member is responsible for the guest's experience from arrival to departure; no issues are passed on.
- **L: Luxury (in the details)** — not excess; small things done consistently well: clean linen, prompt service, a quiet room.
- **D: Distinction** — boutique by design, never generic, never interchangeable with a chain hotel.

## Brand Tone of Voice

*How do we communicate?*

Medallion speaks like a gracious host: warm, composed, and precise. Never loud, never over-promising. Copy should read like a calm, confident welcome: short sentences, no exclamation-heavy hype, no gimmicks. When describing service, be specific and concrete rather than vague ("world-class," "unforgettable"). Let the specificity of the offer create the impression of quality.

**Do:**
- "Your room is quiet and ready."
- "Enjoy freshly pressed linen each night."
- "Breakfast is served at your preferred time."
- "Our staff are always at hand if you need anything."

**Don't:**
- "Experience the most unforgettable stay!"
- "We guarantee world-class service every time!"
- "Lagos' most luxurious hotel!"
- "You will never want to leave!"

**Sample Campaign Copy:**
- "Your room is prepared. Your comfort, assured. Welcome to subtle luxury in Lekki."
- "Return to Lagos and feel truly at home. Your quiet residence awaits at Hotel Medallion."
- "Every detail tended to. Every stay, composed."
- "Morning sunlight. Fresh linen. A gentle start, every day."
- "Choose a hotel where service is personal and space is your own. Choose Hotel Medallion."

**Sample Guest Interactions:**
- **Check-in:** "Good afternoon and welcome to Hotel Medallion. May I help you with your luggage? Your room is prepared and ready for you."
- **Room service:** "Of course. Your breakfast will arrive within 20 minutes. If you would like anything extra, please let me know."
- **Amenities inquiry:** "Our gym and pool are open around the clock for your convenience. Would you like directions or assistance reaching them?"
- **Complaint:** "Thank you for letting us know. I am sorry for the inconvenience. I will take care of this immediately so your stay remains comfortable."
- **Departure:** "Thank you for staying with us. We look forward to welcoming you home again soon."

# Visual Identity Guide

## Logo Colour Guide

The official colour assignment for the Medallion logo and supporting brand system:

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Sky Blue | `#6EC6E8` | Primary brand colour; dominant for the logo mark and primary brand surfaces |
| Lilac | `#B695D6` | Secondary / personality colour |
| Gold | `#C9A227` | Premium accent — medallion mark, foil, key CTAs only |
| Warm White | `#FAF8F3` | Default canvas; background with breathing room |
| Charcoal | `#292929` | Foundation / text colour; wordmark on light, all body copy |

## Suggested Typography

- **Headings:** a refined serif (the "residence" / heritage feel)
- **Body copy:** a clean, highly legible sans-serif (digital and print)
- **Accent/signature only:** a script typeface for the payoff line or select signage, used sparingly

# Stationery & Collateral Specs

**Letterhead** — white or pale sky-blue stock; charcoal body text; gold medallion mark top-left or top-centre; thin lilac or sky-blue rule in the footer with address and contact details below it.

**Business Card** — front: logo centred on white or pale sky-blue card with a lilac hairline border (gold foil or gold-ink medallion mark for premium feel); back: payoff line ("A Quiet Kind of Luxury.") centred, simple.

**Envelope** — matches letterhead; logo top-left, return address in small caps.

**ID Card (Staff)** — vertical format, white or pale sky-blue background; logo top, staff photo centred, name and role beneath in the brand sans-serif; gold accent bar or edge trim; lilac underline for the name/role block; QR or barcode at the base if used for access control.

**Guest-Facing Collateral** — key card sleeves, door hangers ("Do Not Disturb" / "Make Up Room"), restaurant and bar menus all carry the gold medallion symbol at the same size in a fixed position (e.g. top centre) so it reads as a seal, not decoration.
