Clear from the beginning

Good partnerships
start with clarity.

No complicated sales tools. Just a personal link, a private workspace and a commission rate agreed with the hotel.

Who can become a partner?

Travel advisers, businesses, community hosts and people who regularly introduce guests to Lagos are welcome to contact us. Every partnership requires management approval.

How much will I earn?

Your agreed percentage appears in your private workspace. Commission is calculated on eligible room charges before tax, not on the guest's deposit. Rates are agreed individually; they are not a public discount.

When is commission approved?

Only after the guest completes their stay and settles their bill. Cancelled, no-show and refunded bookings do not qualify. Taxes, deposits, food, extras and excluded fees are not commissionable.

How do I track payments and print my records?

Your dashboard shows payment requests, due dates, paid status and the hotel's transfer receipt. Print a dated statement or save it as PDF. Each statement has a verification QR linking to the original hotel-issued record. A statement is not bank confirmation. Share it only with people you trust.

Does my guest pay more?

No. A referral code does not change the room price. Your guest books through the same Hotel Medallion booking process.

Already invited?

Use the email invitation to choose your password, then sign in to your partner dashboard. Share your booking link with guests. Your password and account invitation are private. If a password link expires, request another from the sign-in page.
