The Medallion Comfort

Comfort, considered.

The Comfort room is built on a simple idea: give a guest everything they need and nothing they don't. A queen bed, dressed simply. A work desk positioned where the light actually falls. A private bathroom with both a bath and a shower, so the choice of how to end the day is yours, not the room's.

It looks out onto the garden rather than the street — a small difference that changes how a room sounds in the morning. Coffee and tea are made in the room, breakfast is included downstairs, and the Wi-Fi is fast enough that you won't think about it.

This is the room for a guest who has stayed in enough hotels to know what actually matters, and has stopped asking for anything more than that.

Nothing borrowed. Nothing missing.
